We’ve built a tool called “Slate Setter” that our theatrical team uses to track releases from all
major studios and “wide releases”
1
to help us date our films. The team needs to figure out what
weekends gross well historically (i.e. Valentines Day, Christmas), and what films we’re up
against to maximize film box office.
Additionally, we’re most concerned with movies that might take our audience; for example,
putting our horror film undertone up against Scream 7 might have hurt opening weekend sales.
In addition to when films initially release, they often stay in theaters for many weeks; i.e.
undertone could be up against Week 4 of Scream 7.
Please build a tool that gives teams insight into historical industry performance by week, the
competition (including films “holding over” from previous weeks), and how swapping to different
weeks might change box office performance.
There aren’t very good APIs for this, which is true of many data sources in the film industry. We
expect you’ll likely need to use web scraping, and we’d recommend Box Office Mojo. You also
likely will need scheduling information, about when certain films release “wide”—The Numbers
has accurate information about wide release schedules.
Use any stack you like, we want to see what you can do, your product thinking about what
would be useful to the team, and something that works. Don’t worry about edge cases or
completeness - we want to see a strong proof of concept. Please share source code with us
(@lucasgelfond and @ryanlieu) on GitHub, and deploy your site somewhere. Do not put A24
anywhere in your site’s title, and make sure to remove the deployment after your interview.
